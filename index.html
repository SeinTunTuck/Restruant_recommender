How it works

Hybrid Recommendation

Choose between Item-Based Collaborative Filtering, Location-Aware Recommendation, and Hybrid Recommendation. The selected method controls the ranking shown above.

01Item-Based CF

Compares restaurants by shared rating patterns, then recommends venues similar to restaurants the matched diner liked.

02Location-Aware

Scores nearby restaurants higher while still respecting cuisine, budget, parking, access, alcohol, and payment context.

03Hybrid

Combines Bayesian quality, item/user collaborative taste, preference fit, and travel convenience.

04Comparison

Switch the method to compare how behavior-based, context-based, and hybrid rankings change the shortlist.
